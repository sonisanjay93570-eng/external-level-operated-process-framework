# The External-Level Operated Process Framework

**Proposed by:** Sanjay Soni

## Overview
This repository presents a conceptual interpretative framework that
re-examines the meaning of “self-sustained” or “self-operating” processes
commonly used in natural sciences.

The framework emphasizes that internal processes, while operationally
self-regulated, remain continuously dependent on externally established
boundary conditions.

This work is interpretative in nature and does not propose new physical
laws, mathematical formulations, or experimental predictions.

---

## Purpose
The purpose of this framework is to provide conceptual clarity regarding:
- Causality in natural systems  
- The role of boundary conditions  
- Continuity of processes across scales  

It is intended to coexist with established scientific theories without
replacing or contradicting them.

---

## Scope and Limitations
- No new scientific laws are introduced  
- No experimental claims are made  
- No metaphysical or non-physical assumptions are used  

The framework operates within accepted scientific understanding and
focuses on interpretation rather than theory construction.

---

## Core Idea
Processes commonly described as “self-sustained” are reinterpreted as:
- Internally executed  
- Externally conditioned  

This distinction preserves empirical validity while refining conceptual
framing.
